Community & volunteering

Serve the Cays

Giving back starts with knowing where to begin. I created Serve the Cays to bring Bahamian organizations, volunteer opportunities, and community events into one place.

I lead its design and development through Caynetic, with a simple goal: make it easier to find a cause and take part.

Catch Da Bus

I’m building a place to document Nassau’s jitney routes, stops, and landmarks. Historical references are a starting point; local riders can share route corrections and recent observations for review.

Current routes and service details are still being checked.

CaribbeanSaaS

There’s more being built in our region than people often see. I built CaribbeanSaaS to help people discover software made in or serving the Caribbean, browse by category and region, and visit each product’s own website.
